What to include in an enquiry

A first message that answers these questions usually turns into a quotation in one reply rather than four:

  • Quantity — a single vehicle for a laboratory, or a fleet for a teaching cohort.
  • Delivery country and city — international shipping is a flat US$500 covering shipping only; destination customs clearance, import duties and local taxes are the buyer's responsibility.
  • Institution or individual, and what your procurement process needs — a proforma invoice, a GST number on the quotation, or supplier registration documents.
  • Your target date — a semester start or a RoboRacer / F1TENTH competition date is worth stating explicitly, because lead times depend on component availability at the time of order.
  • Any variant requirement — a specific NVIDIA Jetson or LiDAR variant, or an additional sensor such as a depth camera you intend to power from the AE170 board's spare rails.

Questions

Ordering and support questions

How do I order the RoboRacer Core Kit from within India?

Email the Ambimat Electronics business development team at business.development@ambimat.com, or use the India button in the Order Now section of this page. The Core Kit is INR 580,000 + GST for customer-managed software setup and integration, or INR 628,000 + GST with Software Setup / Technical Support. Flat-rate shipping to any destination within India is INR 4,500 + GST and is charged separately.

How do I order from outside India?

Export orders go through the Ambimat online store at orders.ambimat.com, reachable from the Rest of the World button in the Order Now section. The complete kit is US$5,900, which does not include Software Setup / Technical Support; international customers may request that service separately for an additional fee, with scope and pricing confirmed in the quotation. Flat international shipping is charged separately at US$500 and covers shipping only; destination customs clearance, import duties, local taxes and related destination charges are the buyer's responsibility. Quotations are supplied on FOB terms.

What is the price of the RoboRacer Core Kit, and what does it cover?

In India the Core Kit is INR 580,000 + GST, or INR 628,000 + GST with Software Setup / Technical Support; the INR 48,000 difference is the price of that service. Internationally the complete kit is US$5,900. Shipping is separate: INR 4,500 + GST in India, or a flat US$500 internationally.

Every one of these prices is for the same complete physical kit and its major parts — NVIDIA Jetson Orin Nano Super, VESC 6 MkIV, Hokuyo LiDAR, Traxxas Slash VXL 4x4 chassis, AE170 power distribution unit, MDF base plate, 3S battery and charger. Software Setup / Technical Support — operating-system and ROS stack setup, and 60 days of remote technical support — is included in the INR 628,000 + GST option. The pricing section sets out every line item.

Can I source the chassis, battery and charger locally?

On international orders, yes. In some countries it is easier and more economical to buy the Traxxas chassis, a compatible battery and the corresponding battery charger from a local Traxxas reseller. Excluding exactly those three items from the supplied kit attracts a discount of approximately US$725, giving an indicative price of approximately US$5,175 before shipping. The discount is approximate and the final quotation controls.

The INR 580,000 price, the INR 628,000 price with Software Setup / Technical Support, and the US$5,900 international price all include the chassis, battery and charger.

What should I include in an enquiry?

Quantity, delivery country and city, whether the invoice is to an institution or an individual, your target date, and any specific compute or sensor variant your work requires. Institutional buyers should also say what their procurement process needs — a proforma invoice, a GST number on the quotation, or supplier registration documents.

What technical support is included after delivery?

Where Software Setup / Technical Support is included, remote hardware and software technical support for up to 60 days post delivery, to assist with deployment and setup. That covers bring-up questions such as power-up, network access, ROS 2 topics and driver behaviour. Support requests go to support@ambimat.com, and the first-week diagnostics section of the getting-started guide answers the most common ones directly.

Can Ambimat Electronics supply the power board on its own?

Yes. The AE170 power distribution unit can be ordered separately through the same Order Now routes. Its design files — schematic, Gerber, DXF and bill of materials — are also published as open source for teams that would rather manufacture their own.

Our Story

With 40 years of experience, Ambimat Electronics in Ahmedabad, India, is a single-stop solution enabler to leading PSUs, private sector companies, and start-ups to deliver design capabilities and develop manufacturing capabilities in various industries and markets.

That electronics design and manufacturing background is what sits behind RoboRacer. The AE170 power distribution unit was designed in-house for autonomous racing systems — regulated 19 V, 12 V and 5 V rails from a single 3S pack, feeding an NVIDIA Jetson, a Hokuyo LiDAR and a VESC 6 MkIV — and released as open hardware for the RoboRacer and F1TENTH community.
